A simple, responsive, single-page portfolio designed to introduce myself, showcase my technical skills, share my projects, and provide ways to connect with me.

## Sections

- **Home** – A short introduction.
- **About** – My background, education, and interests.
- **Skills** – Technologies and tools I'm learning and working with.
- **Projects** – A collection of my development projects.
- **Education** – My academic background.
- **Contact** – Ways to connect with me.
